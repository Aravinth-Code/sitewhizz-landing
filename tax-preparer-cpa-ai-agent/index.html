Guardrails

Built to stay away from client tax data

Your AI agent works from your public website and the documents you choose. It isn't connected to your tax software or client portal, so it has no return data to use or disclose.

Answers from your content

Replies come from the pages and files you train it on. When the answer isn't there, it offers to connect the visitor with your office.

Rules you write

Tell it what it must never do, such as ask for an SSN or give tax advice, and what to say instead.

Hand-off to your office

Escalate automatically or when the client asks. You get an email alert, and after hours the client sees your office-hours message.

Separate from your tax software

SiteWhizz never touches returns, client files or your portal. It only sees what visitors type into the chat.
