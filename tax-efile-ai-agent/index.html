Guardrails

Built to stay out of taxpayer data

Your AI agent works from public help content. It has no connection to returns, so there's no return data for it to use or disclose.

Answers from your content

Replies are grounded in the pages and files you train it on. When the answer isn't there, it offers to connect the filer with your team.

Rules you write

Tell it what it must never do, such as ask for an SSN or give tax advice, and what to do instead.

Hand-off to a person

Escalate automatically or when the filer asks. Your team gets an email alert, and filers see a separate message outside your working hours.

Separate from your filing system

SiteWhizz never touches returns, e-file transmissions or IRS acknowledgments. It only sees what filers type into the chat.
