What it does

What your agent does

Each feature exists to get visitors a correct answer, or get them to your team.

An AI agent on your website

Answers visitor questions from your own content while they are on the page, 24/7, so your team sees fewer repeat questions.

Trained on your content

Upload PDFs, docs and URLs. It answers only from that content, names the source it used, and says so when your content does not cover a question.

Easy website embed

One script tag. It works on WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Squarespace, Wix and custom sites, with no coding.

Your look

Set your brand colors, corner position, welcome message and avatar so the widget looks like part of your site.

Human handoff

When it cannot answer, or the visitor asks for a person, it emails your team. Set working hours and an away message.

Conversation history

Read every conversation for 30 days, see which questions were handed off, and export conversations and leads as CSV.
