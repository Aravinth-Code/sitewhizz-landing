Our Story

SiteWhizz was born from a simple observation: businesses spend countless hours answering the same questions repeatedly, while customers wait for responses that could be instant.

We set out to change that by creating AI chatbots that truly understand your business. By training on your website content, documentation, and knowledge base, our AI delivers accurate, helpful responses 24/7.

Today, SiteWhizz helps businesses of all sizes provide exceptional customer support without the overhead, letting teams focus on what matters most - building great products and relationships.
