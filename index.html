How it works

Live on your website in three steps

You don't need a developer or an integration.

1

Add your content

Paste your website URL and upload the docs your team already uses: help articles, FAQs, policies and product guides.

Website URL PDF Word Text
2

Set the rules

Write instructions in plain English: what it must never say, when to hand off, and your team's working hours.

Never quote prices that aren't on the pricing page. If someone asks about their own account, hand off to support.
3

Embed and review

Paste one script tag into your website. It works on WordPress, Squarespace, Wix and most site builders. Read conversations in your dashboard and add the answers it was missing.

<script src="https://app.sitewhizz.io/widget/widget.js"></script>
