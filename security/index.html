Enterprise Security

Your Data Security is
Our Top Priority

We implement industry-leading security practices to protect your business data and ensure your customers' information stays safe.

Built with Security First

Security isn't an afterthought at SiteWhizz - it's built into everything we do. From the ground up, our platform is designed with enterprise-grade security measures that protect your data at every level.

We understand that when you trust us with your customer data and business content, you're placing enormous confidence in our platform. That's why we go above and beyond industry standards to earn and maintain that trust.

  • End-to-end encryption for all data
  • Regular third-party security audits
  • 24/7 security monitoring and incident response
  • Comprehensive employee security training

Comprehensive Security Measures

Multiple layers of protection to keep your data safe at all times.

Data Encryption

All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption and in transit using TLS 1.3, ensuring your information is always protected.

Access Control

Role-based access controls, multi-factor authentication, and single sign-on (SSO) integration for secure team management.

Infrastructure Security

Hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with DDoS protection, WAF, and isolated network environments.

Audit Logging

Comprehensive audit trails for all system activities, with immutable logs stored securely for compliance and forensics.

Backup & Recovery

Automated daily backups with point-in-time recovery, geo-redundant storage, and tested disaster recovery procedures.

Vulnerability Management

Continuous vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, and a responsible disclosure program for security researchers.

Compliance & Certifications

We maintain rigorous compliance standards to meet your regulatory requirements.

SOC 2 Type II

Independently audited security, availability, and confidentiality controls.

GDPR Compliant

Full compliance with EU data protection and privacy regulations.

ISO 27001

Certified information security management system.

CCPA Ready

Compliant with California Consumer Privacy Act requirements.

Data Deletion

Request complete deletion of your data at any time. We honor all requests within 30 days.

Data Export

Export your data in standard formats whenever you need. Your data belongs to you.

No Data Selling

We never sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your Privacy, Protected

We believe in transparency and giving you full control over your data. You always know what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it's used.

Our AI models are designed with privacy in mind. Customer conversations are processed securely and are never used to train public models without explicit consent.
