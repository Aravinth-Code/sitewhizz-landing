Security & Data Handling

How SiteWhizz Handles Your Data

What we store, where it lives, which providers process it, and what you control. If your compliance team needs more detail, ask us directly.

What Your Chatbot Can See

Your chatbot works from the content you give it: website pages, PDFs, documents and text you paste in. It has no access to your other systems, databases or customer accounts.

We store three kinds of data for your account: the content you add, the conversations visitors have with your chatbot, and any lead details visitors choose to share.

  • Answers come only from the content you add
  • Visitors see answers, never your raw documents
  • You write rules for what the chatbot must never ask or say
  • Your data is never sold or used to train public AI models

How We Protect It

The safeguards in place today.

Encryption

Traffic to the website, the dashboard and the chat widget uses HTTPS. Your database and uploaded files are encrypted at rest by our hosting providers.

Sign-in

Sign in with email and password or with Google. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes, never in plain text.

Audit Log

Changes to your account, chatbots and content sources are recorded in an audit log.

Separate Chatbots

Each chatbot answers only from its own content sources, so one site's documents never show up in another site's answers.

Rules You Write

Tell the chatbot what it must never do, such as ask for an account number or give legal advice, and when to hand off to your team.

Report a Vulnerability

Found a security issue? Email admin@sitewhizz.io and we'll look into it right away.

Where Your Data Lives

SiteWhizz runs on these providers. Each one processes your data only to deliver the service.

Provider What it does for SiteWhizz
SupabaseDatabase for accounts, content, conversations and leads
OpenAITurns your content into searchable embeddings and writes chatbot answers. Data sent through OpenAI's API is not used to train their models.
Amazon Web ServicesStorage for uploaded files (S3) and account email (SES)
RailwayHosts the SiteWhizz API
NetlifyHosts the SiteWhizz dashboard
Lemon SqueezyHandles billing and payment details. SiteWhizz never sees your full card number.

SiteWhizz does not hold SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification. If you need a security questionnaire completed, contact us.

Data Deletion

Remove content sources from your dashboard at any time. To delete your whole account and its data, email us and we'll complete it within 30 days.

Data Export

Download your conversations and leads as CSV files from the dashboard.

No Data Selling

We never sell or rent your data, or share it for marketing.

What You Control

You decide what content the chatbot learns from, what it must never say, and when it hands a conversation to your team.

Conversations are used only to run your chatbot and show you what visitors ask. They are never used to train public AI models.
